# Sports fans. Connected campaigns.

Statement Games Media helps brands turn an audience objective into a connected sports campaign. Strategy leads the mix of talent, media, content, events, technology, and fan experiences.

**One accountable relationship, from campaign thinking through coordinated delivery.**

## How we approach the work

- **Start with the audience.** Define the fan, the business objective, and the action that matters.
- **Build the right mix.** Choose the opportunities that serve the campaign, whether focused or spread across several touchpoints.
- **Connect the experience.** Give every part a clear role, from the first impression to the next action.
- **Coordinate and learn.** Align execution and reporting with the objective.

## What this organization supports

Our GitHub organization supports campaign tools, digital experiences, and the operating systems behind our work. Client work and internal records live in private repositories.
